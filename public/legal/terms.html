Terms

Goalden is an accountability tool, not a substitute for judgment.

These terms explain how to treat the early version of Goalden. The short version: use it for your own focus, review AI output, and do not connect sources you do not have the right to use.

Use of the product

Goalden can block or delay access to selected sites, but you remain responsible for your work, deadlines, decisions, and any consequences of relying on the product.

AI output

AI summaries, extracted commitments, and coaching messages may be incomplete or wrong. Before acting on a task, deadline, or next step, review the original source and confirm that it is accurate.

Early access

Goalden is in beta. Features may change, break, or be removed. Pricing, integrations, source support, AI behavior, and extension permissions may change before public launch.

Responsible use

Do not use Goalden to monitor another person without consent, collect private messages from accounts you do not control, or bypass workplace, school, platform, or legal rules.

Contact

For early access, account, or legal questions, use the Gmail link in the landing page footer.
